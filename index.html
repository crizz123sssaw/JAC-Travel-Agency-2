WELCOME TO JAC TRAVEL

Your Mediterranean adventure starts here.

Welcome to your go-to travel website for discovering Granada's Costa Tropical!

Designed by travel enthusiasts, this three-day itinerary connects Motril's sugar heritage on Day 1, the romantic Moorish gardens of Vélez de Benaudalla on Day 2 and the ancient Phoenician coastline of Almuñécar on Day 3.

Whether you are travelling on a tight budget or love taking authentic local products home with you, this route offers culture, nature, gastronomy and relaxation.

TRAVEL YOUR WAY

Two ways to experience the Costa Tropical

Created for travellers aged 25–50.

01

Backpackers

Budget travellers

Travellers looking for authentic and independent adventures. They prioritise public transport, nature walks, free beaches and local tapas culture.

  • Affordable accommodation
  • Free cultural and natural attractions
  • Public transport
  • Budget-friendly meals
02

Shopping Lovers

Food and craft enthusiasts

Travellers who enjoy discovering high-quality local products, traditional food and handmade crafts.

  • Aged local rum
  • Mountain extra virgin olive oil
  • Traditional Moorish-style sweets
  • Local ceramics and tropical fruit
Important note for backpackers

All shopping stops are strictly optional. You can enjoy 100% of the sightseeing, natural spaces and cultural activities without spending money on souvenirs.

THE JOURNEY

Your three-day adventure

Three destinations, three different experiences.

DAY 01

Motril

Sugar heritage, historic centre and Playa Granada

09:30 – 13:30 | Morning Historic centre and sugar heritage
09:30 – 11:00

Plaza de España and the Main Church of La Encarnación

What to do: Explore the Town Hall Square and visit the historic Main Church of La Encarnación, built on the site of an old mosque.

Why visit: It represents the spiritual and architectural heart of Motril.

11:15 – 13:30

Sugar Museum – Former Sugar Factory

What to do: Visit one of the few remaining historic sugar cane processing factories.

Why visit: Discover the centuries of history that made Motril an important centre of sugar production in Europe.

Optional shopping: Sugar cane syrup from the museum shop.
13:30 – 15:30 | Lunch Local food and gastronomy

Lunch and tapas route

Backpackers: Bar La Rueda, near Cerrillo de Motril.

Shopping lovers: Restaurante El Zarcillo, in Las Explanadas, for a relaxed dining experience.

15:30 – 19:30 | Afternoon Panoramic views, local rum and beach relaxation
Getting around Motril: The historic centre and the Sanctuary can be explored on foot, although the walk uphill to the Sanctuary takes extra time. The distillery and Playa Granada are outside the central walking route. Use a local taxi or check local bus connections before departure; allow time for each transfer. Check ALSA services.
15:30 – 16:30

Sanctuary of the Virgen de la Cabeza

Walk through Parque de los Pueblos de América towards the sanctuary and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the coastline.

17:00 – 19:00

Playa Granada

Enjoy a coastal walk, take photographs or relax by the sea. Swimming is weather-dependent and should only be considered when sea conditions are safe.

Cool or rainy weather alternative

Replace swimming with a visit to Motril's historic centre, a local café or an indoor cultural attraction. In windy or rainy conditions, avoid exposed coastal areas.

Optional alternative: Ron Montero Distillery

The distillery's published guided-visit times are not a confirmed 17:00 appointment. Its tours need to be checked and booked directly. Do not include this visit in the fixed afternoon schedule unless the distillery confirms a suitable time for your date.

Official tours, prices and booking information. Book in advance and confirm the language, price and availability.

Optional shopping: Ron Montero products. No purchase is required to enjoy the tour.
20:00 – 23:00 | Evening Dinner and nightlife

Evening entertainment

Dinner: Restaurante Los Moriscos or La Quisquilla.

Nightlife: Chiringuito Hoyo 19 or Pura Vida, beachfront venues with sea views and cocktails.

Overnight stay: Motril

Backpackers: Hostal Costa Andaluza.

Shopping lovers: Hotel Elba Motril Beach & Business Hotel.

DAY 02

Vélez de Benaudalla

Moorish gardens, olive oil and natural gorges

Getting there: Approximately 15 minutes from Motril by regional ALSA bus. Estimated ticket: €1.47.
10:00 – 13:30 | Morning Moorish heritage and castle views
10:00 – 11:45

Jardín Nazarí – Andalusian Garden

What to do: Explore historic water channels, over 500 years old, as well as caves and subtropical vegetation.

Why visit: It is known as the Little Generalife.

12:00 – 13:30

Moorish Quarter and Ulloa Castle

Walk through narrow Mudéjar-style streets until you reach the 16th-century fortified tower, offering views over the Guadalfeo Valley.

Optional shopping: Traditional sweets at Panadería Confitería Molino, including roscos, pestiños and Conchita de San Antón.
13:30 – 15:30 | Lunch Traditional mountain gastronomy

Local restaurants

Backpackers: Restaurante Bar Los Ángeles.

Shopping lovers: Mesón Nacimiento.

What to try: Mountain stews and grilled meats, such as those served at El Surtidor.

15:30 – 18:30 | Afternoon Nature and olive oil traditions
15:30 – 17:00

Nature Walk towards the Vélez River

Enjoy a relaxing nature walk towards the Vélez River, surrounded by beautiful scenery and local landscapes.

Backpacker highlight: A free outdoor activity.
16:00 – 16:45 (subject to opening day)

Andalusian Olive Oil Museum

Discover traditional olive-pressing machinery and learn how high-quality extra virgin olive oil is produced in the surrounding mountains.

Admission: Self-guided visits are listed as free; guided experiences and tastings may carry a fee and require advance booking. Check the winter timetable because afternoon opening varies by weekday.

Check opening hours and contact the museum · Prices and guided experiences

Optional shopping: locally produced extra virgin olive oil.
18:30 – 19:30 | Transfer to Almuñécar Travel from Vélez de Benaudalla to your second overnight stay. Departure time is indicative, not a confirmed bus service.
Plan your transfer: Check the travel date, departure time, journey duration and any connection using the ALSA journey planner. If no convenient direct service is available, arrange a taxi or private transfer. Check routes and book ALSA tickets.

Evening at Bar Los Ángeles

Enjoy a short break near the village's main square before the planned transfer. Keep this stop flexible so you do not miss your confirmed bus or taxi.

Overnight stay: Almuñécar

Backpackers: Hostal Casablanca.

Shopping lovers: Hotel Albayzín del Mar.

DAY 03

Almuñécar

Phoenician heritage, botanical gardens and San Cristóbal Beach

Getting there: Travel from Almuñécar or your accommodation to the town centre. Check the ALSA planner for the actual service, departure time, journey duration and any connection from Vélez de Benaudalla. ALSA journey planner.
08:00 – 09:00

Almuñécar Municipal Market

Discover local produce, fresh seafood and seasonal subtropical fruit. Look for locally made products and regional specialities.

Opening information: Published hours are generally Monday to Saturday, 08:00–15:00. Check holiday closures before visiting; the market is not an afternoon shopping stop.

Municipal Market visitor information

09:30 – 13:30 | Morning Ancient castles and botanical parks
09:30 – 11:30

San Miguel Castle and Archaeological Museum

Visit the Moorish castle on top of the hill and explore the Cueva de los Siete Palacios, with archaeological remains from the area's ancient history.

11:45 – 13:30

El Majuelo Park and Roman Fish-Salting Factory

Stroll among exotic plant species from different parts of the world, surrounded by Roman archaeological remains.

Optional shopping: Handmade ceramics and leather goods from local craft workshops.
13:30 – 15:30 | Lunch Fish, seafood and Mediterranean flavours

Lunch and tapas route

Backpackers: Bar Los Pajaritos, in Cuesta del Castillo, known for fish and seafood tapas.

Shopping lovers: Mesón Los Arcos, specialising in creative Mediterranean dishes.

What to try: Sardine and tropical salad with mango and avocado.

15:30 – 19:30 | Afternoon Coastal views and beach relaxation
15:30 – 16:30

Peñones de San Cristóbal Viewpoint

Walk up to the iconic viewpoint on the rocky peninsula separating the town's main beaches.

16:30 – 19:30

San Cristóbal Beach

Relax by the Mediterranean, swim when sea conditions are safe or enquire about paddleboard and kayak rentals. Availability depends on the season, weather and sea conditions; contact the provider before planning this activity.

Check paddleboard and kayak rental information.

Cool-weather or rainy-day alternative

If the beach is too cold or the sea is rough, explore Almuñécar's historic centre, visit an indoor attraction such as the aquarium (check its opening hours), browse local shops or enjoy a relaxed café stop. Do not schedule water sports in unsafe conditions.

20:00 – 23:00 | Evening Farewell dinner and nightlife

Farewell dinner and entertainment

Dinner: Chiringuito La Dorada de Plata, next to San Cristóbal Beach.

Nightlife: Pub El Convento, an iconic multistory venue in Plaza de la Victoria, offering music and cocktails.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Map and practical information

Everything you need to organise your journey.

Public Bus

Regional ALSA services connect Motril, Vélez de Benaudalla and Almuñécar. Estimated fares range from €1.50 to €3.00 per journey. Check current schedules before travelling.

Travelling by Car

The area is connected by the A-44 and N-340. Road journeys are estimated at around 15–20 minutes between some destinations. Check your route before departure.
